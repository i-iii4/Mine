Power Beyond Realpolitik | Anthropologist Scott Atran explains why so-called "realism" in international relations is psychologically unrealistic: people and countries cooperate, accede, or resist according to their moral commitments.

---

Elsewhere he has noted that the morally more committed side often prevails against a stronger adversary: North Vietnam vs US, Afghanistan vs Russia and then US; Israel in 1948; perhaps the American colonists ("our sacred honor") vs Britain.

---

A moral commitment to a rules-based international order might command more loyalty and solidarity than passivity in response to raw bullying.
