Art on Contract: last year I wrote an interactive tool to generate ASCII-art for @titlesxyz’s series of smart contracts. 

A short thread
↓

![](art-on-contract-last-year-i-wrote-an-interactive-tool-to-generate-ascii-art-for-img0.mp4)

---

The tool outputs text files with a comment block that can be added to the headers of smart contracts. These comments are visible in the contract source code on @etherscan.
↓

![](art-on-contract-last-year-i-wrote-an-interactive-tool-to-generate-ascii-art-for-img1.jpg)

---

The shapes are SDF-based and some of the parameters are exposed to the UI. 
↓

![](art-on-contract-last-year-i-wrote-an-interactive-tool-to-generate-ascii-art-for-img2.jpg)

---

The rendering and shading are achieved through dithering using ASCII block elements.
